# How to Check Battery Voltage (Elenco LCM-1950)

1.  **Setting the Multimeter:**
    *   Look for the **V** section with a straight line `V—` or `DCV` (Direct Current Voltage).
    *   Turn the dial to **20** (if available) or **40**.
        *   **Why?** Your battery is ~7.4V.
        *   If you choose **4**, it's too low (overload).
        *   If you choose **400**, it's safe but less precise.
        *   **Do not use V~ (ACV)**.

2.  **Connecting Probes:**
    *   **Black Probe** -> **COM** port.
    *   **Red Probe** -> **VΩ** port.

3.  **Measruing:**
    *   Touch the **Red Probe** to the metal part inside the **Red wire's** slot on the Deans connector.
    *   Touch the **Black Probe** to the **Black wire's** slot.
    *   **Reading:** You should see a number on the screen.
        *   **> 7.0V**: Good charge.
        *   **< 6.0V**: Battery is dead/empty and needs charging.
        *   **Negative number?**: You just swapped red/black probes (harmless).

**Safety:** Be careful not to let the two probes touch each other while they are touching the battery connector (short circuit risk).
